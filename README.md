COSMOS – Space Research Intelligence

COSMOS is an AI-powered space research platform that uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and previously collected research data to help users explore research papers, discover knowledge, analyze research trends, and identify connections between different space-science topics.

Key Features

NLP-Based Search: Understands natural-language queries and finds relevant research information.

Research Discovery: Explore research papers, authors, publication details, abstracts, and topics.

Semantic Search: Finds relevant information based on meaning and context.

Research Universe: Explore connections between research topics, technologies, and methods.

Global Research Analysis: View research distribution across different countries.

Trend Detection: Identify emerging, increasing, and declining research topics.

Previous Research Data: Uses an existing research dataset to support information discovery and analysis.
